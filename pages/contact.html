Common Questions

How do I report an incorrect shortcut?

Email owner@fastshortcuts.com. Include which shortcut is wrong, which tool page it is on, and your OS (Windows, macOS, or Linux).

Can you add shortcuts for [tool name]?

Yes. Email the tool name and, if you have it, a link to its official shortcut docs to owner@fastshortcuts.com.

Do you accept contributions?

Yes. Open an issue or pull request on GitHub, or email the change to owner@fastshortcuts.com.

Why haven't you responded to my message?

Check your spam folder first - sometimes our replies end up there. If it's been more than a week, please resend your message. We promise we're not ignoring you; emails occasionally slip through the cracks.

Can I sponsor a placement on FastShortcuts?

Yes. There is one labeled homepage slot at $50 for 3 months. Email owner@fastshortcuts.com with your product and a short description. Paid links will use rel="sponsored". Shortcut guides stay free and unchanged.

How can I support FastShortcuts?

The best support is spreading the word! Share FastShortcuts with your dev team, tweet about it, or mention it in your blog. The more people who use it, the more motivated we are to keep improving it.
