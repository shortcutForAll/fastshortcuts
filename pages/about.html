About FastShortcuts

An independent keyboard shortcut reference. Each cheat sheet is compiled from official vendor documentation and kept next to a working search box. Guides stay free; any paid placement is labeled.

Who runs this

FastShortcuts is an independent project published at fastshortcuts.com. It is not a company blog and it is not anonymous spam: the site has a public source repo, a working email, and a documented method for how shortcuts get onto a page.

Email: owner@fastshortcuts.com

Treat the email and GitHub account as the people behind the site — not a fictional editorial team.

How shortcuts are verified

Every cheat sheet is compiled from that tool’s official keyboard-shortcut documentation (linked in the page footer). Platform tabs show Windows/Linux and macOS separately when the vendor ships different defaults. When a vendor changes a default — Cursor, Warp, Claude Code, and similar tools do this often — the page is updated and the visible “last verified” date is refreshed where it exists.

If a shortcut on the site does not match what you see in the app, email owner@fastshortcuts.com with the tool, the OS, and the official doc URL if you have it.

Why Keyboard Shortcuts Matter

Here's the thing about shortcuts: they're not just about speed (though that's a nice bonus). They're about maintaining focus.

Studies show it takes 23 minutes to fully recover from an interruption. Every time you stop to google a shortcut, you're not just losing those 30 seconds - you're potentially losing 20+ minutes of deep work.

Keyboard shortcuts let you stay in the zone. Your hands never leave the keyboard. Your eyes stay on the code. Your brain stays in flow state. That's when the magic happens.

Learn the 15–20 shortcuts you actually use in your main tool first. That is enough to stay in the keyboard most of the day.

What Makes FastShortcuts Different

There are other shortcut resources out there. Here's why we think FastShortcuts is better:

🔍 Instantly Searchable: Every page has live search. Type what you want to do, find the shortcut instantly. No scrolling through hundreds of irrelevant commands.
